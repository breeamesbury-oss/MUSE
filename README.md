MUSE 
This is a tunepad project, to use this project you will need to import the json into tunepad, to hear the 4 pieces.
About Muse: Muse was created as a journaling tool, for individuals who can't express themselves with words. 
Meaning that they would turn to 'Muse' a musical composition piece, to express and validate their feelings.
Muse is composed of four musical pieces.
SAD: This sound whilst its peaceful, as a heavier meaning to it, for someone who is struggling. 
ANGRY: This is a punchy and hard hitting piece, reflecting the harsher music with emotion. 
ANXIOUS: This mirrors a heart beat, with panic rising through climbs in the music. 
CALM: This reflects the ambience of the ocean, the piece replicates a wave.
